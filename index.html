More than a name

For Michael “Murph” Murphy.

Michael J. Murphy served the Milwaukee County Sheriff's Office for 25 years. He met a 19-year fight with lymphoma with the same loyalty, humour and quiet strength he brought to his family and community.

He and his cousin always talked about opening an Irish pub called Murphy's Law. Today, that idea lives here in Lagoa—a family place built for good food, football, conversation and the easy camaraderie a real pub should have.

Family-owned Tanya Murphy, Michael's wife, and Liam Murphy, his son, are owners of Murphy's Law and carry that dream forward.

“May the road rise to meet you, and the wind be always at your back.”
